Step 1: The Global Clock (Sync the Time) ⏲️
What it does: Someone clicks "Start Game," and the server begins a 60-second countdown for everyone.
Outcome: Everyone sees the same number (59... 58... 57...) at the top of the canvas at the exact same time.
Step 2: The Word Mystery (Artist vs. Guessers) 📝
What it does: The server picks one player to be the Artist.
The Artist sees the word: "APPLE"
Everyone else sees the blanks: _ _ _ _ _
Outcome: The Artist can't "unintentionally" reveal the word in chat, and it creates the central mystery of the game.
Step 3: Guessing & Scoring Logic (The "Winning" Part) 🏆
What it does: The server listens to the chat. If a message matches the word:
The message is hidden from others (so they don't cheat!).
That player gets Points (e.g., 50 pts).
The Artist gets a small bonus too!
Outcome: The Players sidebar updates live, showing the "Leader" rising to the top (#1, #2, #3).
Step 4: The Game Loop & Ending (The "Repeat" Part) 🔄
What it does: When the timer hits 0:
Reveal the word to everyone.
Clear the board automatically for the next round.
Switch the Artist to the next person in the list.
End Game: After X rounds, show a big "WINNER" screen with a podium!
